About

Fundamentals first.

I'm a finance student at Northeastern's D'Amore-McKim School of Business. I like taking a company apart from its filings and working out how it funds itself, how much room it has before that funding comes under stress, and what that means for its lenders and investors. This site is where I do that work outside the classroom.

This past summer I was an accounting summer analyst at The Turtle Group, reconciling property-level accounts and building an Excel payroll forecast. I was also a financial planning summer analyst at Oak International, where I built a pricing and order model. As a junior analyst in the Northeastern Real Estate Club, I underwrote real estate investments and built DCF models alongside Zanzibar Capital.
